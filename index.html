Troubleshooting

If nothing appears, check that no other app is using the camera, that this site has camera permission in your browser's address bar, and that the page is opened over HTTPS. If the preview is blocked inside an embedded frame, open the page in its own tab.

Video call resolutions

ResolutionSizeMicrosoft TeamsZoomGoogle MeetSlackThis camera

Testing briefly turns the camera off and on, and tries each size exactly. Both apps send a lower resolution when bandwidth or the meeting layout requires it. In Zoom, 720p and 1080p depend on your account and settings (1080p has to be enabled by Zoom Support). Google Meet lets you pick the send resolution (360p, 720p or 1080p) in its video settings. Slack's 4K support is as you told me; its help pages only list bandwidth needs (about 600 kbps up and down for a two-person video huddle).
